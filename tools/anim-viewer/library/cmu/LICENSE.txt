CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database
http://mocap.cs.cmu.edu

"The data used in this project was obtained from mocap.cs.cmu.edu.
The database was created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217."

CMU places no restrictions on the use of the original dataset. These files come from the BVH conversion by
Bruce Hahne (cgspeed), who places no additional restrictions on it, via the mirror at
https://github.com/Shriinivas/cmubvh.

Each .bvh here is one take, named by its CMU id (subject_trial), cut to a short window and resampled from
120 to 30 fps by tools/anim-viewer/cmu_import.py, which lists the window of every take.

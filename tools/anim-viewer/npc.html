NPC clip viewer

The race clip files PulseLib plays in game (geckolib/animations/entity/npc_biped_<race>.animation.json, built by node tools/anim-viewer/build_npc.js), on the test figure or an exported Middle-earth NPC. Held items are scaled to their class length (weapon_grips.json) about the fist and follow the weapon bones. The list shows the clips an NPC plays with its weapon (attacks fall back between classes as in game).

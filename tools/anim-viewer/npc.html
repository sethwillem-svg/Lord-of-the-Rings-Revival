NPC clip viewer

The race clip files PulseLib plays in game (geckolib/animations/entity/npc_biped_<race>.animation.json, built by node tools/anim-viewer/build_npc.js), on the test figure or an exported Middle-earth NPC. Held items are sized by the drawn weapon to their class length (weapon_grips.json, pommel to tip), seated with the handle on the fist, and follow the weapon bones; shields are strapped to the forearm, face out from its back (gecko/shield.js, as in game); bows are held upright across the forearm, string toward the archer, and in the bow clips the string is drawn as the game shows it (gecko/bow.js: the line from the tips to the nock, and the model shown in the time bar). The list shows the clips an NPC plays with its weapon (attacks fall back between classes as in game).
